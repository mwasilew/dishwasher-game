    Everything must fit. Everything must come out clean.

    How to play

    Load every dish into the two racks, then close the door and run it. The machine is the judge: if the racks won't slide home, or a mug is sitting the wrong way up, or a plate is shading a glass from the spray arms — you'll hear about it.

    When it won't run

    Nothing is thrown away. The racks come back out packed exactly as you left them, and whatever the machine objected to is marked in red — pick it up and the mark clears. Let me fix it takes you back to the load; Start over empties the machine, and you only need that if you want it.

    The rules of a good load

    • Cups, bowls and pots face down. Anything left facing up fills with water.
    • Nothing sticks out. The racks have to roll in and the door has to shut.
    • Don't block the spray arms. Keep things above the rack floor — a pan handle poking down jams the arm.
    • Don't shadow other dishes. Water travels in straight lines. A big tray leaning over the glasses leaves them dirty.
    • Load it where it belongs. Plates, pots and trays go in the lower rack where the spray is strongest; cups, glasses and light plastic go up top. The tray marks each item ▼ or ▲.
